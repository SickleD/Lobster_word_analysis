Artificial intelligence is a branch of computer science that aims to create intelligent machines.
Machine learning is a core part of artificial intelligence that enables computers to learn from data.
Deep learning is a subset of machine learning based on artificial neural networks.
Natural language processing is a field of artificial intelligence and linguistics.
Computer vision is the science of making machines see and understand visual information.
Applications of artificial intelligence include robotics, speech recognition, image recognition, and expert systems.
Artificial intelligence has been applied in many domains including healthcare, finance, education, and transportation.
The future of artificial intelligence holds great promise for solving complex problems.
